## Why this version asks questions instead of only asking for money

A district office is not a funder in its own right — it is the gateway to the
provincial programmes and it holds the extension officers. An email that asks
"which programme, which window, which officer, which documents" gets a reply that
tells you exactly how to apply. An email that only asks for R455 700 gets filed.

The three questions also quietly establish that you know CASP and Ilima/Letsema
exist, which is not true of most applications that land on that desk.
