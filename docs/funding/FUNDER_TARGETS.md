# Where to send the Tlhago Agri Pro proposal

**Important:** no contact detail below has been verified. This environment's network
policy blocks every outbound site, so none of these addresses, portals or cycles
could be checked. Treat this as a list of *institutions and programmes* — get the
current contact details from each organisation's own website or office.

---

## Read this first: do not send it to everyone

Three reasons a mass send costs more than it earns:

1. **Most of the money below is not applied for by email.** CASP, Ilima/Letsema,
   NYDA and the Blended Finance Scheme run on application forms with opening and
   closing dates. An unsolicited email to a general inbox is not an application and
   does not start the clock.
2. **You usually get one attempt per cycle.** A rejected application often cannot be
   resubmitted until the next window. Sending before the land documents exist
   spends that attempt for nothing.
3. **Unsolicited bulk email to strangers is spam.** It gets deleted unread, and it
   can get `shakukabelo2@gmail.com` flagged — which then affects the emails you
   actually need delivered.

Ten well-aimed applications will beat two hundred cold emails, and the shortlist
below is genuinely short because the profile — youth-owned, communal land,
Limpopo, first commercial season — points at a specific set of doors.

## The gate: land tenure

Most Tier 1 funders check proof of land rights at screening, before anyone reads
the business case. Right now the proposal says the land "has been offered" by the
traditional authority. That is not a document.

**Get a Permission to Occupy (PTO) or a signed written lease from the Chief and
the Induna before you submit anything.** It is the first objective in your own
proposal for exactly this reason. This single piece of paper will do more for the
application than any amount of redrafting.

## Tier 1 — Government (the best fit for this project)

Start provincially, not nationally. CASP and Ilima/Letsema are administered by the
province, and the national department will route you back there anyway.

| Target | Why it fits | How to approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Limpopo Dept of Agriculture & Rural Development (LDARD)** | Administers CASP and Ilima/Letsema in your province | **Start here** |
| **Your Lepelle-Nkumpi extension officer** | The single highest-value contact you have | In person, at the municipal or district office |
| **CASP** (Comprehensive Agricultural Support Programme) | Infrastructure and equipment for smallholders — matches your R377 250 equipment line almost exactly | Through LDARD |
| **Ilima/Letsema** | Production inputs — matches your R42 450 seed/fertiliser/pesticide line | Through LDARD |
| **Blended Finance Scheme** (National Dept of Agriculture + Land Bank) | Grant/loan blend for black producers | National department |
| **Mafisa** | Micro agricultural finance | Through accredited intermediaries |
| **AgriBEE Fund** | Transformation funding in agriculture | National department |
| **NYDA Grant Programme** | Youth-owned business grants — you are a young farmer, this is a direct fit | NYDA branch or portal |
| **SEDFA** (formerly SEFA/SEDA) | Small enterprise finance and support | Provincial office |
| **Land Bank** | Commercial agricultural finance | **Hold** until the PTO/lease exists — they will ask first |
| **NEF / IDC** | Larger tickets; better suited to your Phase 2–3 agro-processing plans | Later, not now |

## Tier 2 — Corporate supplier development (the warm door)

This is the route most people in your position overlook, and you are unusually
well placed for it because you already name your buyers.

- **Spar and Shoprite/U-Save enterprise & supplier development.** You list both as
  intended retailers. Their ESD programmes exist specifically to capitalise
  emerging suppliers, and the retailer earns B-BBEE scorecard points for doing it.
  Approach the ESD or transformation desk, not the store.
- **Massmart supplier development programme.**
- **Mining Social & Labour Plans.** Limpopo mining operations are legally required
  to spend on local community development in their host areas. Anglo American,
  Exxaro and the platinum-belt operations all run these. Ask the community
  development officer at the nearest operation what their SLP covers.
- **Old Mutual Masisizane Fund** — agriculture-focused SMME funding.
- **SAB Foundation** — Tholoana Enterprise Programme.
- **Bank enterprise development desks** — Standard Bank, FNB, Absa, Nedbank all
  run agri and ESD programmes.

## Tier 3 — Development organisations

Technoserve, Solidaridad, and similar programmes run smallholder market-access
projects in South Africa. They rarely give cash but do give agronomy, market
linkage and sometimes equipment — all of which reduce what you need to raise.
